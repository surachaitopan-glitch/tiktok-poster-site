Privacy Policy – Shopee AI Poster

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What this app is

Shopee AI Poster is a personal tool, built and used by a single individual, to prepare videos and posts and upload them as drafts to that same person's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What we access

When the owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives: the account's Open ID, basic profile information (user.info.basic), and an access token and refresh token that allow uploading draft videos (video.upload).

How it is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.
  • Tokens are stored in a file on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server, not shared, not sold, and not used for advertising or analytics.
  • This website only forwards the TikTok authorization response to the owner's computer. It stores and logs nothing.

Sharing

We do not share TikTok data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps, and can delete the local token file. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the issues page of the GitHub repository that hosts this site.
